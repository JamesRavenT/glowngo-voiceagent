01 / About

Good hair shouldn't cost you an afternoon of phone tag.

Glow & Go opened in Silver Lake in 2019 with three chairs and one idea: the hardest part of a haircut shouldn't be booking it. Four salons later, that idea still runs the place.

Our stylists specialize rather than generalize. Nova paints balayage. Dmitri cuts. Yuki fixes color that went wrong somewhere else. When you book, you're booking a person, not a slot.

Booking happens by voice. Tell our agent what you want and when, and it finds the stylist who can actually do it — checking real availability, not adding you to a waitlist.

04 / FAQ

Questions, interrupted.

How do I book an appointment?

Talk with our voice agent. It checks live availability and books the time that works for you.

Is this a real salon?

No. Glow & Go is a demonstration built by James Raven Tabag. Every booking is synthetic, and no one is expecting you.

Can I request a specific stylist?

Yes. Ask for a stylist by name, or ask for the first available stylist.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Tell the voice agent your booking reference code. That code is required to change or cancel an appointment.

What is the cancellation policy?

Please give at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule.

How long will my appointment take?

Services run from 15 minutes to 4 hours. The agent will confirm the duration when you book.

Do you take walk-ins?

We are appointment only. Ask the agent about same-day openings.

Do I need a consultation for color correction?

Yes. A consultation is required before you can book color correction.

Where can I see my booking?

You can view it on the public sheet, which carries synthetic demo data only.
