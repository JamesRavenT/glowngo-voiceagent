## Traps found while planning

Each of these will silently break something if the implementer isn't told.

- **`page.test.tsx:36` asserts `heroHeadlineLines.join(" ") === tagline`.** "Meet your next look"
  splits to `["Meet your", "next look."]`, so `tagline` must become `"Meet your next look."` — with
  the period.
- **`Navbar.png` is 500×500; `Navbar Text.png` is 612×408.** Different aspect ratios. The navbar
  hardcodes `width={162} height={64}` at `w-32` in an `h-20` bar — dropping the square mark in at
  that width renders a 128px logo in an 80px bar. This is the likely cause of the clipped hamburger.
- **Serving both navbar assets by CSS visibility downloads both on every device.** Use `<picture>` or
  media queries, not two hidden `<Image>`s.
- **`contactCopy.floatingCallButton` is both visible text and `aria-label`,** asserted by four e2e
  specs. Splitting them is required, not optional — see [design.md](../design.md#accessibility).
- **Removing the footer disclaimer breaks a standing honesty requirement** unless the hero
  disclaimer carries attribution. [ADR 0004](../decisions/0004-disclaimer-placement.md).
- **Service categories reach the agent, not just the UI.** `content/` feeds
  `generate-knowledge-base.ts`. Regenerate and re-upload.
- **Full-height panels are a content budget.** Locations at 4 branches × 7 days of hours does not fit
  a 2×2 grid at 100svh. Hours collapse to grouped ranges; services drop descriptions. Both survive in
  the knowledge base.
- **GSAP snap needs an offset-based `snapTo` function,** not `1/(n-1)`. Equal panels make the simple
  form work today and break silently when one section changes height.

## Verification

- **Per chunk:** lint, type-check, build, relevant unit tests (Codex); Claude reviews and commits.
- **Design gate:** James picks one of five directions before chunk 10.
- **Playwright (release gate):** existing suite, updated for the new labels, the hero disclaimer, and
  the centered modal. Runs with reduced motion — snap animation is a known flake source.
- **Manual:** click through at desktop and mobile widths; confirm snap feel, carousel-inside-snap on
  a real touch device, and that the badge and disclaimer are visible.

## Risks

- **Snap scrolling is the highest-risk chunk.** It interacts with the navbar's IntersectionObserver,
  the floating button's scroll listener, the carousels, and the Playwright gate. If something
  regresses, it starts here.
- **Carousel inside a vertically-snapped page is the specific thing most likely to feel wrong on
  touch.** Embla avoids the nested-scroll-container conflict by design
  ([ADR 0002](../decisions/0002-embla-for-carousels.md)), but this needs a real device, not emulation.
- **"Fashionista but minimalist, futuristic but not overflashy" is a taste judgment, not a spec.**
  Hence five directions and an explicit gate rather than one attempt.
